⚖️ Katarungang Pambarangay Guidelines

📌 Covered Cases Under Lupon Authority

  • Boundary line disputes and property encroachments between neighbors.
  • Unpaid personal loans, financial obligations, or minor collection claims.
  • Community disturbances (excessive noise, unruly pets, neighborhood nuisance).
  • Verbal altercations, slander, oral defamation, or slight physical injuries.
  • Civil disputes between individuals residing in the same city or municipality not classified as serious criminal offenses.

⛔ Cases Excluded from Barangay Conciliation

  • Offenses punishable by imprisonment exceeding one (1) year or fines over PHP 5,000.
  • Complaints involving public officers or government employees relating to the performance of official duties.
  • Disputes where one or both parties reside in different provinces or distant municipalities.
  • Disputes involving real property located outside the jurisdiction of the city or municipality.

🔄 The 3 Stages of Katarungang Pambarangay

  • Stage 1 (Mediation by Punong Barangay): The Punong Barangay summons both parties within fifteen (15) days to achieve an amicable settlement.
  • Stage 2 (Pangkat Conciliation): If mediation fails, a three-member panel (Pangkat Tagapagkasundo) is constituted to resolve the matter within another fifteen (15) days.
  • Stage 3 (Settlement or Certificate to File Action): If agreed upon, an Amicable Settlement is signed with the force and effect of a final court judgment. If conciliation fails, a Certificate to File Action (CFA) is issued to allow formal court filing.

🔒 Data Privacy & Confidentiality (RA 10173)

All personal information submitted through LuponResolve is handled with strict confidentiality under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) and will be used solely for the official conciliation proceedings of Barangay Sumilihon.
