Katarungang Pambarangay Guidelines (RA 7160)

1. Filing & Approval Workflow

  • Step 1 (Screening): Complete the Preliminary Jurisdiction Screening to verify if the dispute is within Barangay authority.
  • Step 2 (Report & Evidence): Submit your complaint details along with optional photo evidence and your Gmail address.
  • Step 3 (Lupon Approval & Account): Once approved by an officer, use your Tracking Docket Number to register a Resident Account.

2. Three Stages of Conciliation

  • Mediation by Punong Barangay: Initial settlement conference within 15 days.
  • Conciliation by Pangkat Tagapagkasundo: 3-member panel hearing if mediation fails.
  • Certificate to File Action (CFA): Issued if no amicable settlement is reached, allowing court filing.
