⚖️ Katarungang Pambarangay Guidelines

📌 Covered Cases Under Lupon Authority

  • Boundary line disputes and property encroachments between neighbors.
  • Unpaid personal loans, financial obligations, or minor collection claims.
  • Community disturbances (excessive noise, unruly pets, neighborhood nuisance).
  • Verbal altercations, slander, oral defamation, or slight physical injuries.
  • Civil disputes between individuals residing in the same city or municipality.

⛔ Cases Excluded from Barangay Conciliation

  • Offenses punishable by imprisonment exceeding one (1) year or fines over PHP 5,000.
  • Complaints involving public officers relating to official duties.
  • Disputes where parties reside in different provinces or distant municipalities.

🔄 The 3 Stages of Conciliation

  • Stage 1 (Mediation by Punong Barangay): 15-day amicable settlement period.
  • Stage 2 (Pangkat Conciliation): 3-member panel conciliation within 15 days.
  • Stage 3 (Settlement or CFA): Formal settlement or Certificate to File Action in Court.
